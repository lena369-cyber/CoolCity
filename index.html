[ 02 // THE PHENOMENON ]

WHAT IS AN URBAN HEAT ISLAND?

Urban Heat Islands (UHIs) occur when dense concentrations of asphalt roads, concrete towers, and non-reflective bitumen roofs absorb solar energy during the day and re-radiate it slowly throughout the night, trapping sweltering heat pockets.

[ 01 ]

Low Albedo Thermal Mass

Standard asphalt streets absorb 95% of incoming solar radiation. At solar noon, dark road surfaces routinely exceed 65°C (150°F), turning entire downtown districts into thermal heat batteries.

[ 02 ]

Canopy & Soil Deficit

Replacing trees and porous soil with impervious pavement completely halts natural evaporative cooling (evapotranspiration). A single mature tree transpires up to 400 liters of water daily, acting like a 10-room air conditioner.

[ 03 ]

Waste Heat Trapping

Air conditioning units, traffic exhaust, and industrial machinery vent massive quantities of anthropogenic waste heat directly into tight street canyons, generating a self-reinforcing heating feedback loop.

[ CLARIFICATIONS ]

FREQUENTLY ANSWERED INQUIRIES

How quickly can a cool roof reduce surface temperatures? +

Immediately upon application. A high-albedo elastomeric white coating reflects up to 88% of solar irradiance on day one, reducing peak afternoon roof surface temperature from 70°C to less than 38°C instantly.

How does COOLCITY calculate district heat reduction? +

We synthesize empirical data from NASA Landsat-9 thermal infrared sensors, municipal ground-level IoT weather nodes, and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) models of urban canyons to simulate microclimate cooling.

What is the difference between air temperature and surface temperature? +

Surface temperature measures how hot materials like asphalt and concrete become under direct solar radiation (often reaching 65°C+). Air temperature measures ambient air at pedestrian level. Cooling the surface prevents heat from transferring into the ambient air.

Can individual tenants or homeowners participate? +

Yes! Individual homeowners can paint flat roofs white, install permeable driveway pavers, plant native shade trees on south-facing perimeters, and participate in our weekly Citizen Sensor Patrol walks.
